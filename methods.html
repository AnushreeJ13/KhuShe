Contraceptive Methods

Birth Control Pills

99% effectiveness

Daily oral contraceptive pills that prevent pregnancy by stopping ovulation and thickening cervical mucus.

Pros

  • Highly effective when taken correctly
  • Can make periods lighter and more regular
  • May improve acne

Cons

  • Must take at the same time daily
  • Possible side effects like nausea
  • Doesn't protect against STIs
Learn More

IUD (Intrauterine Device)

99% effectiveness

A small T-shaped device inserted into the uterus that provides long-term pregnancy prevention.

Pros

  • Lasts 3-10 years
  • Highly effective immediately
  • Low maintenance

Cons

  • Requires insertion by a doctor
  • Possible cramping after insertion
  • May cause irregular bleeding
Learn More

Contraceptive Implant

99% effectiveness

A small rod inserted under the skin of the upper arm that releases hormones to prevent pregnancy.

Pros

  • Lasts up to 3 years
  • Highly effective immediately
  • Discreet and low maintenance

Cons

  • Requires minor procedure
  • May cause irregular bleeding
  • Possible side effects
Learn More

Condoms

85% effectiveness

Barrier method that covers the penis or lines the vagina to prevent pregnancy and STIs.

Pros

  • Protects against STIs
  • No hormones
  • Widely available

Cons

  • Must use correctly every time
  • Can break or slip
  • May reduce sensation

Contraceptive Patch

91% effectiveness

A small sticky patch worn on the skin that releases hormones to prevent pregnancy.

Pros

  • Changed weekly
  • Easy to use
  • May regulate periods

Cons

  • Visible on skin
  • Possible skin irritation
  • Same side effects as pill

Vaginal Ring

91% effectiveness

A flexible ring inserted into the vagina that releases hormones to prevent pregnancy.

Pros

  • Changed monthly
  • Discreet
  • May regulate periods

Cons

  • May cause vaginal irritation
  • Possible side effects
  • Must remember to replace

Sterilization

99% effectiveness

A permanent surgical procedure for those who are certain they do not want future pregnancies.

Pros

  • Permanently prevents pregnancy
  • One-time procedure
  • No ongoing maintenance

Cons

  • Requires surgery
  • Not reversible in most cases
  • No STI protection

Fertility Awareness

76% effectiveness

Natural family planning method where you track your cycle and avoid sex during fertile days.

Pros

  • No side effects
  • Cost-effective
  • Empowering for body awareness

Cons

  • Less effective than other methods
  • Requires daily tracking
  • No STI protection

Emergency Contraception

89% effectiveness

Pills taken after unprotected sex to prevent pregnancy. Most effective within 72 hours.

Pros

  • Easy to access
  • Can prevent pregnancy after contraceptive failure
  • Non-invasive

Cons

  • Not for regular use
  • May cause nausea
  • No STI protection
