Expert Care FAQs

Answers to common questions about our premium expert services

How do I schedule a 1-on-1 consultation?

Once you've subscribed to Premium or VIP membership, you can book consultations directly through your dashboard. Select your preferred expert, choose an available time slot, and you'll receive confirmation details via email.

What's the difference between group webinars and 1-on-1 sessions?

Group webinars are live educational sessions where you can learn from experts along with other members (with Q&A time). 1-on-1 sessions are private consultations focused entirely on your personal health situation and questions.

Can I change my membership plan later?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes will take effect at your next billing cycle, with prorated charges or credits applied as needed.

Are the consultations covered by insurance?

While we don't directly bill insurance, many of our members successfully submit receipts for reimbursement. We provide detailed invoices with necessary codes for you to submit to your provider.
