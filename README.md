# Khushe: A Holistic Digital Health Companion for Women

**Website:** [Khushe](https://khushe.vercel.app/)
**Demo Video:** [Watch Demo](https://vimeo.com/1076629015)

---

## 🌸 Problem Statement

Today’s women’s health apps focus narrowly on fertility or menstruation, leaving over 60% of a woman’s health journey unsupported.

* Millions of girls enter puberty unprepared.
* Midlife women face hormonal chaos with no structured help.
* Post-menopausal women are largely invisible in the digital health space.

**👉 One-size-fits-all doesn’t work. Women’s needs evolve — but digital health hasn’t caught up.**

---

## 💡 Solution: Khushe

**Khushe** is a comprehensive digital health platform that supports women across all life phases, offering tools and guidance tailored to their evolving needs.

---

## 🌟 Key Features by Life Stage

### Adolescence

* **PCOS Plate** → Track and manage symptoms related to PCOS
* **BodyBrave** → Build body confidence and positive self-image
* **Period Tracker** → Stay informed and organized through menstrual cycles
* **SafeHer** → Resources and tips for personal safety

### Reproductive Years

* **PillPicker** → Find the right contraceptive pill
* **Ovulation Tracker** → Plan for or prevent pregnancy
* **Contraceptive Options** → Understand methods beyond pills
* **Explore QnA** → Community-driven questions and answers

### Perimenopause

* **Menopause Tracker** → Monitor symptoms, hormonal changes, and health trends

### Wisdom Years (Post-Menopause)

* **Bone Health** → Tips and tracking to maintain strong bones
* **Heart Health** → Heart disease prevention and awareness
* **Brain Health** → Support cognitive wellness

### For All Ages

* **Breastie** → Breast cancer awareness, self-check guides, and screening reminders
* **Gynae Connect** → Connect with gynecologists for consultations
* **PharmNav Forum** → Navigate pharmacies, medications, and discussions with peers
