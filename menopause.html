Common Triggers ▾

Record daily symptoms to identify your personal triggers.

Common triggers for menopausal symptoms include:

  • Stress and anxiety
  • Caffeine
  • Alcohol
  • Spicy foods
  • Hot environments
  • Lack of sleep

Pattern Analysis ▾

Continue tracking your symptoms daily to reveal patterns in your data.

The app will automatically analyze your entries to help you identify:

  • Which times of day your symptoms are most severe
  • How your symptoms correlate with stress levels
  • Whether certain activities help reduce symptoms
  • How your menopause journey is progressing over time
